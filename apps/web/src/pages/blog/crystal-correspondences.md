The second world, **Crystal Garden**, is a collection of stones, tools and garden corners. Each
mosaic's lore line borrows from the correspondences crystal lovers have passed around for
generations. These are traditions and inspiration, not promises, but they make lovely company
while you sort colors.

## Amethyst

The violet stone of calm and clear intuition, often kept by the bed or the meditation cushion. It
opens the world as _Amethyst Point_, a single proud spire.

## Rose Quartz

The soft pink stone of love, and especially of loving yourself. _Rose Quartz Heart_ shows it as a
heart, with a few bright facets catching the light.

## Citrine

Golden and sunny, linked to warmth, confidence and abundance, which is why its mosaic is the
_Citrine Sun_.

## Clear Quartz

Known as the "master healer" and an amplifier for other stones and intentions; here it stands tall
as the _Clear Quartz Tower_. In the game, clear and pale colors often sit next to stronger ones,
which is exactly when layered colors get tricky.

## Pendulums, grids and wands

Not every mosaic is a stone. The _Pendulum_, the _Crystal Grid_, the _Selenite Wand_ and the
_Crystal Ball_ are the tools and small rituals around the crystals that make the garden feel lived
in.

## Why the Crystal Garden is harder

This is where **layered colors** arrive: fragments of one color hiding others behind them. A wisp
collects only the first fragment it sees, so the order you launch wisps in matters more here.
Take your time: there are no timers, and Retry is always free.
