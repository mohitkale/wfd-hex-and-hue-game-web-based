The third chapter of the witch's journey, **Crystals**, is a small collection of stones and the
tools kept with them. Each picture's lore line borrows from the correspondences crystal lovers have
passed around for generations. These are traditions and inspiration, not promises, but they make
lovely company while you sort colors.

## Clear Quartz

Known as the "master healer" and an amplifier for other stones and intentions. It opens the
chapter as _Clear Quartz_, a single clear point that takes on whatever you set into it.

## Amethyst

The violet stone of calm and clear intuition, often kept by the bed or the meditation cushion.
_Amethyst_ shows a cluster of its points.

## Rose Quartz and Citrine

Two more stones every crystal shelf seems to have: rose quartz, the soft pink stone of love, and
especially of loving yourself; and citrine, golden and sunny, linked to warmth, confidence and
abundance.

## The crystal ball

Not every picture is a stone. _Crystal Ball_ is the scryer's tool: a glass full of slow purple
weather, where small stars drift and settle.

## Charging a grid

The chapter ends with _Charge_, a crystal grid: stones laid out in a pattern and charged together,
so that each one strengthens the others.

## Why this chapter is harder

This is where the order of your picks starts to matter. Colors sit inside other colors, and a box
whose color is still buried waits in its tray. Take your time: there are no timers, and Retry is
always free.
