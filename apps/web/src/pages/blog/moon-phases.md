The fourth chapter of the witch's journey, **Moon & Stars**, follows the moon through its cycle.
Many witches and moon-watchers plan their intentions around it, and each phase carries its own
mood. Here is how we read them while drawing the pictures.

## New Moon

The sky is dark and the slate is clean. It is a time for quiet planning and for deciding what you
want to grow. In the game, it is the moment before your first tap: the whole picture is still
waiting to be revealed.

## Waxing Crescent

The first sliver of light returns. Traditionally this is when you set an intention and take a
first small step toward it. That is why the chapter opens with _Crescent_, a single curve of moon.

## First Quarter

Half lit and growing. This phase is about decisions and pushing through the first obstacles, the
point where a plan meets real life.

## Waxing Gibbous

Almost full. A time to refine and adjust: you can see the shape of the result, but it still needs
care. If you have ever had every tray waiting on a colour that is still buried, you know the
feeling.

## Full Moon

Everything is illuminated. The full moon is linked to culmination, gratitude and celebration, and
to charging crystals in its light. In Hex & Hue, it is the reveal: the finished picture blooming
into your Spellbook.

## Waning Gibbous, Last Quarter and Waning Crescent

As the light shrinks, the traditions turn to sharing what you learned, releasing what no longer
serves you, and finally resting before the next cycle begins.

## The whole cycle at once

_Triple Moon_ puts waxing, full and waning side by side, the old sign of the cycle as a whole.
_Owl & Moon_ keeps watch under it, and the chapter ends with _As Above_: a night sky that carries
the old saying "as above, so below".
