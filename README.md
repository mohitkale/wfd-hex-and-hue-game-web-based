# Hex & Hue: Magic Pixel Puzzle

_A Little Magic in Every Color._

A free-to-play magical color-sorting puzzle for the web. Summon Wisps, send them around the mosaic to
collect fragments of their color, manage your holding slots, and clear the board to reveal a magical
illustration for your Spellbook.
