# Hex & Hue: Magic Pixel Puzzle

_A Little Magic in Every Color._

A free-to-play magical color-sorting puzzle for the web. Summon groups of familiars that fetch, carry
and deliver the cubes of a magical picture, manage your holding slots so no group is left stranded, and
finish the picture to reveal its lore in your Spellbook.
